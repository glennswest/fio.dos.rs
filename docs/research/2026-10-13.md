## 2. Specs
- FAT32 itself unchanged. Reported (secondary sources, unverified): Windows 11 Insider builds raised the `format` FAT32 cap from 32 GB to 2 TB
  ([Storage Newsletter](https://www.storagenewsletter.com/2026/06/19/microsoft-breaks-past-fat32s-32gb-barrier-extending-support-up-to-2tb/)).
  The spec never had that limit; it only means large FAT32 volumes will be more common. Our 2 GiB FAT32 test does not cover multi-hundred-GB volumes (FAT size, cluster-count edge cases).
- No change found to the exFAT spec ([MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/windows/win32/fileio/exfat-specification)) or to LFN rules.
